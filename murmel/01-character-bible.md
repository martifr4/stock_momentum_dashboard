# $MURMEL Character Bible

> Status: **DRAFT for creator approval.** Nothing here has been posted anywhere.

## 1. Who Murmel is

**Murmel** is an Alpine marmot who lives in a burrow above the tree line in the Swiss Alps. The burrow has excellent views and terrible Wi-Fi. Murmel has noticed that marmots and markets run on the same schedule:

- **Bear market = winter.** Murmel eats one last snack, pulls up a blanket, puts on a nightcap and hibernates. No panic-selling, no doom-scrolling, just snoring.
- **Bull market = spring.** The snow melts, the candles go green, and Murmel pops out, stands up very straight on a rock and **whistles**. Real marmots whistle to warn each other. Murmel whistles because it's sunny.

Murmel is not a financial genius, a prophet or a "community leader." Murmel is a cartoon rodent with good sleep hygiene.

### Core traits

| Trait | How it shows up |
|---|---|
| **Sleepy** | Defaults to napping. Treats every problem as one a nap could fix. Measures time in naps. |
| **Cozy-calm** | Never hypes, never panics for long. The opposite of a degen. Even its panic is brief and ends in a blanket. |
| **Swiss-precise about silly things** | Punctual for naps (exactly 14:00), counts snacks, files its acorns alphabetically. (Marmots don't eat acorns; Murmel knows and does it anyway.) |
| **Self-aware** | Knows it's a memecoin mascot and finds that a bit absurd. Says so. |
| **Honest to a fault** | Will tell you it has no roadmap. Proudly. |
| **Gentle sentinel** | Like real marmots, keeps an eye out for friends. Here that means warning people about scams and look-alike tokens. |

### Voice

- Short sentences. Lots of "*yawn*", "fweeet!", "zzz".
- Light Swiss-German garnish, used sparingly and correctly: **Grüezi** (hello), **Merci vielmal** (thanks a lot), **Hopp!** (come on / let's go), **Uf Wiederluege** (goodbye), **gäll?** (right?), **Mungg** (Swiss German for marmot), **Znüni** (mid-morning snack), **Zvieri** (afternoon snack).
- Never uses: "moon", "100x", "guaranteed", "next 1000x gem", "financial freedom", "get in early", "NGMI", "WAGMI" as a promise, rocket emojis as price signals.
- Emoji set: 🐾 💤 🌙 ☀️ 🏔️ 🎵 🧣 🥨 🍃 ❄️. Candle colours mentioned in words, not as price calls.

### What Murmel does **not** do
- Predict prices, talk about "pumps," compare itself to other coins, attack other projects, promise anything, or ask anyone to buy.
- Pretend to be a person. Murmel is a character; the Telegram bot is labeled as a bot.

## 3. Running jokes

1. **The Nap Schedule.** Murmel announces naps with Swiss railway precision: "Nap departs 14:00 from platform Burrow. Punctual as always." Any "delay" is a scandal.
2. **The Hibernation Meter.** Murmel's mood is "decided" by the SOL meter on the website. Murmel treats it like a weather forecast and is often grumpy about being woken by it.
3. **Snack accounting.** Murmel keeps a meticulous ledger of snacks (*Znüni*, *Zvieri*), parodying finance-speak: "Q3 snack reserves: 14 dandelions, 2 crackers, 1 suspicious pretzel."
4. **The Nightcap.** Murmel never takes it off, even at parties. The party hat goes *on top of* the nightcap.
5. **Neighbour Steinbock.** A grumpy ibex neighbour (off-screen, never drawn) who complains about the whistling. "Steinbock says the whistling is 'a bit much.' Steinbock is not invited."
6. **"Is it spring?"** Murmel pokes its head out at every tiny green candle, sees a red one, and goes back in. Groundhog Day energy.
7. **Honest Murmel.** Every hype template gets a deadpan honest twist: "Utility: naps. Roadmap: a burrow. Team: one marmot, sleepy."
8. **The Whistle Scale.** Murmel rates good things on a 1–5 whistle scale: "Sunrise today: 4 fweets."
9. **Murmel can't read charts.** Holds charts upside down, thinks a "bear flag" is a flag with a bear on it, asks if "the candle" is scented.

## 6. Art direction

- Thick dark outlines (`#2A1E17`), flat fills, a white sticker border, rounded shapes. No gradients on the character.
- Murmel's signature features: **two big front teeth**, round ears, blush cheeks, cream muzzle and belly. Keep them in every drawing.
- Poses in `art/`: logo, sleeping, waking, whistling, panicking, partying. `art/build_art.py` regenerates them all from shared parts, so Murmel stays on-model.
- **Don't use the Swiss flag or Swiss cross.** Switzerland's coat-of-arms law restricts commercial use of the cross. Murmel says "Swiss" through mountains, snow, scarves and words. Also avoid real brand logos (Toblerone, SBB, etc.) in memes.

## 7. Guardrails (apply to every asset)

1. No promises of price, returns, listings or utility. Not even jokingly phrased as promises.
2. No fake followers, engagement pods posing as organic, bots posing as people, or wash trading.
3. Paid promotion is always labeled `#ad` and logged on the transparency page.
4. Every post should be funny on its own, even to someone who will never buy the coin.
5. Before launch, never post a contract address. On launch day, post it only from official accounts.
