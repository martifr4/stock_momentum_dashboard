- TikTok: these are meme and character content. Check TikTok's current rules on financial or crypto promotion before posting anything that mentions the coin, and keep the coin mentions light. The character can carry the account by itself.
- Suggested bio link: the website (contract address shows "not launched yet" until launch day).
