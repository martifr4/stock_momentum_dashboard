Markets

How to read this. Every tracked market is classified into themes (e.g. “Central banks & rates”), each with a direction (“easier policy”) and hand-set sector sensitivities. A market's YES-probability move × its direction × sensitivity × a volume weight gives the pressure on each sector; multi-outcome events count once, and markets about to expire are damped. Scores are relative (weighted probability points), for spotting where odds are shifting — not investment advice. Tune themes & sensitivities in backend/markets/impact_map.py.
